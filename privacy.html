LIBAS: The Legend of Arif — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026. Developer: Fahmi.

LIBAS does not collect any personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and the developer receives no information about you or how you play.

Your game progress

Saves and settings stay on your device. They are not sent to the developer.

Purchases

The full game is unlocked with an in-app purchase handled by Apple (or Google on Android). The store processes your payment under its own privacy policy. The developer never sees your payment details.

Children

LIBAS collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the change reaches the game.
